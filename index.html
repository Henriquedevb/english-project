Thanksgiving: Turkeys, Traditions & TV

The wild (and slightly awkward) history of America's favorite food holiday — plus the sitcoms that turned it into a running joke.

What is Thanksgiving, exactly? 🦃

  • A U.S. (and Canadian) holiday celebrated on the fourth Thursday of November.
  • Officially: a day to give thanks for the harvest and the year gone by.
  • Unofficially: a day to eat an enormous amount of food with your family.
  • Centers on a big dinner, a parade, football on TV, and — for many families — a little bit of drama.

Think of it as Christmas dinner's laid-back older cousin who shows up a month early with a turkey instead of presents.

The "first Thanksgiving" (1621) 🌽

  • Autumn 1621: English colonists in Plymouth (the "Pilgrims") held a harvest celebration.
  • About 90 Wampanoag people joined 52 English colonists for the feast.
  • The Pilgrims had a brutal first year — half of them died. They survived mostly thanks to the Wampanoag, who taught them to grow corn, fish, and find edible plants.
  • A Wampanoag man named Tisquantum (Squanto) acted as translator and guide between the two groups.

Plot twist: nobody in 1621 called it "the First Thanksgiving" — that name (and the friendly, simplified story) was mostly created centuries later.

Henrique

From local feast to national holiday 📜

  • For over 200 years, Thanksgiving was just a regional New England tradition — every state picked its own date.
  • Sarah Josepha Hale (magazine editor, and yes, she also wrote "Mary Had a Little Lamb") campaigned for 17 years to make it official.
  • 1863: President Abraham Lincoln proclaimed a national Thanksgiving Day — in the middle of the Civil War.
  • 1939: FDR moved the date earlier to boost Christmas shopping. People hated it and nicknamed it "Franksgiving." Congress fixed the date to the fourth Thursday of November in 1941.
Karol

Modern traditions 🏈🎈

  • The dinner: turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce, pumpkin pie.
  • Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in NYC — running since 1924, giant balloons and all.
  • Football: the NFL has played games on Thanksgiving since the 1920s.
  • Black Friday: the massive shopping day right after — basically Thanksgiving's chaotic sequel.

Thanksgiving on TV 📺

Thanksgiving dinners going wrong is basically its own sitcom genre.

Everybody Hates Chris — "Everybody Hates Thanksgiving" (S2E8)

Julius tries to out-cook his successful brother, forgets to put the turkey in the oven, and dinner finally happens... at 1:30am. Chris then gives a Thanksgiving history speech at school that gets awkwardly honest.

Friends — "The One with All the Thanksgivings"

A flashback episode covering the gang's worst Thanksgivings ever — including Monica's toe and Brad Pitt's grudge against Rachel.

WKRP in Cincinnati — "Turkeys Away"

A radio station drops live turkeys from a helicopter as a promotion. "As God is my witness, I thought turkeys could fly."

Brooklyn Nine-Nine — the yearly heist

Every Thanksgiving, the detectives compete in an elaborate heist game instead of, you know, working.
